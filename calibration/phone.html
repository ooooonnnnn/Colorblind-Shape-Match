Measure the screen

Best: the Meter appIt reads the camera's raw sensor data, so the phone's picture processing can't spoil the measuring. Small (about 25 KB), straight from this screen.

The first time this downloads the app: open the download to install it (allow installing from this browser if asked), then come back here and tap the button again. Download it again

or measure with this page:

1
Dim the roomNo lamp or window shining on the screen.
2
Hold it closeStraight in front of the screen, camera facing it. A little blurry is good.
3
Circle between the ringsMove closer or back until the bright circle's edge is between the two dashed rings and they turn green.
4
Keep stillAbout two minutes. Resting the phone on something helps.
